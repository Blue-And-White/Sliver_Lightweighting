# Shared Room contract

The Room has one authoritative Goal and two shared graphs.

- Intel contains reusable evidence-backed facts and capabilities.
- Exploration contains active Intents, attempts, outcomes, handoffs, and useful negative results.
- Read shared state before starting substantial work.
- Publish material results early enough for another Session to continue them.
- Keep guesses and ownership out of Intel; keep full transcripts out of both graphs.
- External acceptance is the completion authority. Chat prose is not.
